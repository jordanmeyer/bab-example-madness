Reading the evidence

Forecast percentages are a team’s chance of reaching each stage from the selected snapshot. They are not its chance of winning only the next game. A checkmark means the stage was already reached; a dash means zero chance.

How this was built

The request: recreate the archived tournament dashboard, with a selector for every year from 2016 onward.

The implementation uses native HTML for an accessible bracket and table, Apache ECharts for team forecast history, and locally bundled data and fonts. Complete opening fields from each available source are fitted to a bracket model, then updated using completed results. An equal-source average and a prior-season logistic blend are available where the data support them. FiveThirtyEight’s source selection uses its published historical snapshots; other sources and both blends use reconstructed updates.
