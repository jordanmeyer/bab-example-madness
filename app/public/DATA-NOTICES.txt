Tournament Atlas — data attribution and transformations

FiveThirtyEight: original men’s and women’s NCAA forecast CSVs, 2016–2019 and 2021–2023, recovered from the Internet Archive. Data licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International as stated at https://github.com/fivethirtyeight/data/blob/master/README.md . License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ and the adjacent LICENSE-fivethirtyeight-data.txt. Changes: men’s and women’s rows separated, columns renamed, conversion to JSON, documented 2018 LIU ID/name correction. Each year JSON retains its exact archive URL and source digest. Model probabilities are preserved, not refitted.

Bart Torvik: pre-tournament T-Rank Time Machine ratings dated 2024-03-18, 2025-03-17 and 2026-03-16. Sources: https://barttorvik.com/timemachine/team_results/YYYYMMDD_team_results.json.gz . Public bulk-data guidance: https://adamcwisports.blogspot.com/p/data.html . Model explanation: https://adamcwisports.blogspot.com/p/every-possession-counts.html . Only the tournament teams’ rating facts and name joins are retained. No formal CC-BY/MIT license is claimed for these inputs. Our separate reconstruction applies the documented neutral-court log5 formula and exact bracket propagation; it is not a tournament forecast published by Bart Torvik. Ratings are held fixed and no tournament results enter the calculation. No daily replay is invented.

ESPN: limited historical tournament facts (teams, seeds, regions, dates, scores, winners, status) for both competitions, extracted from March/April NCAA scoreboards 2016–2026. Each year/game retains its source URL. No article text, images, school logos, publisher code or betting data are copied. ESPN facts are not represented as FiveThirtyEight-licensed data.

NCAA: championship history cross-check and 2018 printable bracket used to verify LIU’s 16 seed; 2020 cancellation explicitly retained. https://www.ncaa.com/history/basketball-men/d1 . These sources are not represented as MIT or CC-BY licensed site content.

Oregon–VCU 2021: the no-contest retains Oregon’s advancement and null scores, replacing administrative 1–0 markers. It is not counted as a played game.

This independent educational example is not affiliated with the data publishers, NCAA or any university. Library/font notices are provided separately in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt.

Women’s extension: 74 original FiveThirtyEight snapshots, 2016–2019 and 2021–2023; 650 actual tournament games across ten played seasons through 2026. ESPN host-site labels (including Iowa City in 2019) are reconciled to the archived bracket regions, with per-game corrections retained. Women’s 2024–2026 have results only; no pre-tournament probability model is claimed. Women’s championship cross-check: https://www.ncaa.com/history/basketball-women/d1 .
